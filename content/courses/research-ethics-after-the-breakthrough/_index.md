**Code:** REAB502

**Level:** Postgraduate

**Teaching period:** Spring 2026

**Contribution:** Guest seminar series and discussion materials

**Prerequisites:** None

**Instructor:** Dr Victor Heinrich Frankenstein

What happens when an experiment succeeds in a way its creators did not anticipate? This seminar examines duty of care, responsibility for consequences and the limits of “we followed the protocol” as a complete ethical defence. Cases are fictional, but the questions are intended to feel familiar.

## Learning outcomes

Identify affected parties; distinguish consent from ongoing care; evaluate foreseeable and unexpected consequences; propose a responsibility plan; explain when an experiment should stop.

## Illustrative assessment design

Case analysis, 50%; duty-of-care plan, 30%; seminar reflection, 20%.
