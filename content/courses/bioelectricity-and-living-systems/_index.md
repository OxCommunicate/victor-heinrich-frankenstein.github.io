**Code:** BELS401

**Level:** Advanced undergraduate / introductory postgraduate

**Teaching period:** Autumn 2026

**Contribution:** Practical-session design and small-group facilitation

**Prerequisites:** Introductory physiology and basic quantitative methods

**Instructor:** Dr Victor Heinrich Frankenstein

This course introduces bioelectric signalling as a problem of biological organisation. Students examine how signals are measured, how experiments are designed, and why striking observations require careful interpretation. The emphasis is on reasoning from evidence rather than achieving a suitably dramatic laboratory atmosphere.

## Learning outcomes

Explain basic bioelectric signalling; distinguish immediate responses from sustained function; identify confounding variables; propose a controlled experiment; communicate uncertainty clearly.

## Illustrative assessment design

Experimental-design proposal, 40%; critical reading exercise, 30%; final explanatory essay, 30%.
