title: Designing a repeatable experiment
weight: 30
type: docs
---

Reproducibility starts before data collection. Record the experimental conditions, specify exclusions in advance, and separate exploratory questions from confirmatory claims. An observation that cannot be repeated may still be interesting, but it requires a different description from a robust finding.

Assignment: write a one-page experimental plan with a research question, controls, outcome measures and stopping criteria. “Wait for lightning” is not an adequate methods section.
