This fictional lecture considers the gap between producing an outcome and taking responsibility for it. Drawing on the themes of “Responsible Innovation in Private Laboratories”, Victor argues that monitoring, support and accountability should be designed into ambitious research from the outset. The closing question is less “Can we do this?” than “Who will still be here when we have?”
