This month marks three years since I joined the Geneva Institute for Experimental Science as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow. My research question remains modest in wording and somewhat less modest in implication: can damaged biological systems be restored to full and independent function?

The fellowship has given me space to develop that question, publish early findings and learn from colleagues who are commendably willing to ask whether my interpretation is actually supported by the result.

Building an independent research profile is not simply a matter of having an interesting idea. It also involves careful methods, clear writing, reliable collaborations and discovering how much can be accomplished before the next funding deadline.

The laboratory notebook is better organised than it was three years ago. The soundproofing is not. I consider this a balanced account of progress.
