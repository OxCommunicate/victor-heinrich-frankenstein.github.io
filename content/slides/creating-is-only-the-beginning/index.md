## Creating Is Only the Beginning

Victor Heinrich Frankenstein. Geneva Early Career Research Forum. 17 May 2026. *Fictional presentation for the OxCommunicate website showcase.*

---

## The exciting question

Can we make this work? What would count as success? Which observations would change our minds?

---

## The neglected question

Who is affected after the experiment ends? What support might they need? Who remains responsible?

---

## Permission and care

Approval establishes conditions for beginning. Monitoring responds to what actually happens. Duty of care continues when the protocol no longer supplies an easy answer.

---

## Design the morning after

Name the responsible person. Specify monitoring and stopping criteria. Plan communication and support. Record decisions and review them.

---

## Three warning signs

“We will work out the consequences later.” “The result is too exciting to pause.” “Surely somebody else will take over.”

---

## A better definition of success

A result that is well supported. A method others can inspect. A team that remains accountable. An outcome that does not depend on its creator disappearing.

---

## Questions

What obligations would your next experiment create? Who would notice if they were unmet? Thank you. Please leave the pitchforks in the umbrella stand.
