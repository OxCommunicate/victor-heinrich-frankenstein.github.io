**Status:** Ongoing postdoctoral project

**Start:** 2024-09-01

Creator Responsibility examines what researchers owe to the people and systems affected by their interventions. Rather than treating ethics as a gate passed before work begins, the programme considers responsibility across the full life of an experiment.

Its teaching and discussion materials explore monitoring, communication, unintended consequences and the allocation of responsibility when a project changes direction. The central question is deliberately uncomfortable: who remains accountable when the exciting part is over?

The programme informs “Responsible Innovation in Private Laboratories” and Victor’s current teaching contributions. Its unofficial motto is “Do not flee the laboratory and call it a handover.”
