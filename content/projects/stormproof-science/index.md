**Status:** Completed academic project

**Start:** 2020-01-15 · **End:** 2023-08-31

Stormproof Science was Victor’s doctoral methods project, developing a consistent approach to experimental records, instrument checks and replication. The programme responded to a familiar problem: an exciting result is of limited use if its decisive conditions exist only in the investigator's memory.

The project introduced structured logs, explicit stopping rules and a distinction between exploration and confirmation. It also encouraged the reporting of negative results, which were filed under “useful evidence” rather than “unfortunate paperwork”.

The associated conference paper, “Scaling Challenges in Regenerative Anatomy”, considers how scale affects experimental design and repeatability. No public software repository or dataset is supplied for this showcase.
