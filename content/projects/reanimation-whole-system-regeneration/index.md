**Status:** Ongoing postdoctoral project

**Start:** 2023-09-01

Victor’s current project investigates whether coordinated electrical, chemical, and anatomical interventions can restore activity across an entire biological organism.

The project brings together techniques from surgery, chemistry, electrical engineering, and materials preservation. Early results have been sufficiently energetic to justify further investigation.

Due to the sensitivity of the fictional research, detailed methods, laboratory locations, and information concerning research participants are not currently available to the public.

This is Victor’s principal fictional postdoctoral research strand, developed within his fellowship. Its associated fictional publications are “Toward a General Theory of Vital Restoration” and “Galvanic Activation in Complex Anatomical Systems”. Neither the site nor its downloadable citations supplies real reanimation procedures or evidence of such an achievement.
